Orbital plane (face-on)

Plane of the sky

Drag the planet (pink) along the orbit to changee E, the periastron marker (orange) to change ω, or the ascending-node marker (blue) to change Ω. The Thiele-Innes elements only change when the orbit changes, not when the planet moves. In RV 'mode' the ascending node is the one where the body recedes from the observer.
