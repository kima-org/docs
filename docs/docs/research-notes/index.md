# Research Notes

This is a collection of informal research notes on exoplanet detection and data
analysis. They present ongoing exploration of techniques, algorithms, and
observations, mostly related to the radial velocity method and to **kima**. The
notes have not been peer-reviewed, but each note has a DOI on Zenodo and can be
cited. The goal is to foster discussion, experimentation, and learning, so
corrections, questions, and ideas are very welcome: please start a [discussion
on GitHub](https://github.com/kima-org/kima/discussions).
